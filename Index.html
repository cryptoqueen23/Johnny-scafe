Our Family Story

Good food, friends and family, just like Grandma did it. Johnny's Family Cafe & Catering carries on a Central Texas tradition built around feeding people and bringing families together.

The food may bring you through the door, but the goal is simple: make you feel like you've pulled up a chair at the family table.

Come hungry. Stay awhile. Become part of the Johnny's family.
